Type

Project Summary

Harmonix Health engaged me to develop their complete brand identity and build their digital platform for a medical device distribution company. This comprehensive project involved creating a cohesive visual identity system, designing user-centered interfaces, and developing a robust website that effectively communicates their medical device distribution services.

The project focused on establishing Harmonix Health as a trusted authority in the medical device distribution space through strategic brand positioning and a seamless digital experience. I worked closely with their team to understand their target market and create a brand that resonates with healthcare professionals and medical facilities.

The final deliverables included a complete brand guidelines package, logo system, color palette, typography selections, and a fully responsive website optimized for user engagement and conversion in the medical device distribution industry.
