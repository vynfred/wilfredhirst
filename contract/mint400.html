The Mint400 project involved comprehensive content creation and coordination for one of the most prestigious off-road racing events in North America. Working closely with the event organizers, I managed the production of promotional materials, race documentation, and digital content strategy.

This high-energy project required coordinating multiple content streams including photography, videography, and social media assets. The work encompassed pre-race promotional content, live event coverage, and post-race highlight materials that captured the excitement and intensity of desert racing.

The content created helped amplify the Mint400's brand presence and provided engaging materials for sponsors, participants, and racing enthusiasts worldwide, contributing to the event's continued growth and recognition in the motorsports community.
