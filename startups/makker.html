Startup: Makker Clothing

Dates Live
2015 - 2017
Type
D2C Fashion
Role
Founder

Makker Clothing began with a successful Kickstarter campaign for a unique magnetic keychain product. This initial success grew into a comprehensive wallet and sustainable clothing D2C company focused on creating high-quality, ethically-sourced apparel with innovative design elements.

The company specialized in minimalist designs with maximum impact, using eco-friendly materials and transparent supply chain practices. Starting with the magnetic keychain innovation, we expanded into wallets and eventually a full sustainable clothing line that resonated with conscious consumers.

Through the initial Kickstarter success and innovative product development, we grew from a single product concept to a recognized brand in the sustainable fashion space, serving customers across North America with our unique magnetic accessories and sustainable apparel.
