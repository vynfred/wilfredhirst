Startup: Deadi

Dates Live
2021 - 2023
Type
Productivity Platform
Role
Co-Founder & Product Lead

Deadi was an innovative productivity platform designed to help individuals and teams manage deadlines, tasks, and project timelines more effectively. Our mission was to eliminate the stress of missed deadlines through intelligent planning and automation.

The platform featured advanced scheduling algorithms, collaborative tools, and predictive analytics to help users stay on track with their commitments. We integrated with popular productivity tools and provided insights to improve time management skills.

During its operational period, Deadi served thousands of users across various industries, helping them improve their productivity and reduce deadline-related stress through our comprehensive platform.
