Startup: BlueSkyATG

Dates Live
2024
Type
Website / SEO
Role
Development / Customer Success

BlueSkyATG is a technology company focused on supporting website development and creating innovative tools for D2C brands. I contributed to the development of their generative garment tool, which enables fashion brands to test designs without upfront production costs.

The generative garment tool represents a breakthrough in fashion technology, allowing brands to visualize and iterate on designs digitally before committing to physical production. This reduces waste, speeds up the design process, and enables more sustainable fashion practices.

My role involved both technical development and ongoing customer success initiatives, ensuring that clients could effectively utilize the platform to enhance their design workflows and achieve better business outcomes through reduced risk and faster time-to-market.
