Startup: Vynfred

Dates Live
2017 - Present
Type
D2C
Role
Artist / Founder

Vynfred is a direct-to-consumer print store specializing in original artwork and creative designs. The platform showcases and sells unique artistic compositions, from abstract digital art to intricate geometric patterns, all created by the founder.

The store focuses on high-quality print-on-demand products, offering customers the ability to purchase original artwork on various mediums including prints, apparel, and home goods. Each piece represents a unique artistic vision translated into accessible consumer products.

Since its launch in 2017, Vynfred has built a dedicated following of art enthusiasts and collectors, establishing itself as a recognized brand in the digital art and print-on-demand space with international shipping and a growing catalog of original designs.
