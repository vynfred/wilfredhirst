Startup: Philly2illy

Dates Live
2025
Type
SEO/AIO, Web Development
Role
Web Designer & SEO Specialist

Designed and developed a comprehensive website for Philly2illy, a Boston-based DJ specializing in high-energy performances and event entertainment. The project focused on creating a modern, visually striking online presence that captures the dynamic nature of DJ culture while maintaining professional credibility for booking inquiries.

Implemented comprehensive SEO strategies to improve search visibility for local DJ services in the Boston market. This included keyword optimization for terms like "Boston DJ," "wedding DJ Boston," and "corporate event entertainment," along with local business schema markup and Google My Business optimization to enhance local search presence.

Utilized Firebase hosting for reliable, fast-loading performance with global CDN distribution. The platform choice ensured excellent uptime, quick deployment capabilities, and seamless integration with modern web technologies, providing an optimal user experience for potential clients browsing the DJ's portfolio and services.
